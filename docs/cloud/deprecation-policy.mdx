Temporal may deprecate generally available features.
This policy covers changes to code-facing functionality or existing size, rate, retention, concurrency, quota, or other service limits when previously valid usage could fail or require customer action.

For such changes, Temporal will:

- Provide affected customers at least **6 months’ notice before the change is enforced**, consistent with the [Temporal Server versions and support policy](/temporal-service/temporal-server#versions-and-support).
- State the affected functionality or limit, enforcement date, expected impact, consequences of inaction, and required customer actions.
- Provide migration and validation steps, compatibility requirements, limitations, and a supported replacement or mitigation where practical.
- Notify identifiable affected customers directly by email or in the Temporal Cloud UI, with updates in documentation or the [changelog](https://temporal.io/changelog/product-area/cloud).

Preview or experimental capabilities may change or be withdrawn with less notice, as described in their applicable terms or documentation.
Temporal may also use a shorter notice period when necessary to address urgent security, legal, service-integrity, or third-party dependency issues.
In those cases, Temporal will provide as much notice and assistance as reasonably practicable.

Changes to UI layout, styles, and wording intended for human judgment are outside this policy and may occur without notice.
Temporal Labs features are outside of this policy and may be changed in incompatible ways at any time.
